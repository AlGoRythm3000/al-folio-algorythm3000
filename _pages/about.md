I am a machine learning student. I hold a master's degree from
[ENS Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/) in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering,
and I am currently enrolled in the [IASD](https://www.masteriasd.eu/) master's in Artificial Intelligence,
Machine Learning and Data Science ([ENS Ulm](https://www.ens.psl.eu/) / [Mines Paris](https://www.minesparis.psl.eu/) /
[Université Paris Dauphine-PSL](https://dauphine.psl.eu/)). I was also admitted to the French
[*Agrégation*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agr%C3%A9gation) in Engineering and Computer Sciences (national rank 15/151).

From April to September 2027, I will be a **Research Intern at [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/) (Spatial AI Lab Zurich)**,
studying the cone effect and the modality gap in text-vision embedding models (CLIP, SigLIP). I am looking for a
**PhD position in robotics and computer vision** starting in October 2027.

The topic I worked the most on so far has been **geometric and topological deep learning** &mdash; graph neural networks,
rewiring and lifting techniques, and the oversmoothing / oversquashing problems &mdash; as a research assistant at
[INRIA Saclay](https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-saclay-centre) and [CentraleSupélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/)
(March&ndash;August 2026). This work led to two manuscripts in preparation. I have also worked on applied machine
learning for tax-fraud estimation at the [French Ministry of the Economy and Finance](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgfip), on RNN-based
control of an exoskeleton at [Hublex](https://www.hublex.co/), on post-seismic damage detection
with YOLO at the [University of Pavia](https://web-en.unipv.it/), and on computer vision and
reinforcement learning for autonomous racing.

Outside research I build [Edge-AI](/al-folio-algorythm3000/projects/) pipelines on single-board
computers, play the flute, race triathlons, and teach maths, physics and computer science.

See my [CV](/al-folio-algorythm3000/cv/) for the full story, or get in touch by
[email](mailto:gabriel.franco@ens-paris-saclay.fr).
