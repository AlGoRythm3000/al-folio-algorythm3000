I joined **INRIA Saclay** and the **CentraleSupélec** lab as a research assistant, working on
oversmoothing and oversquashing in graph learning through *rewiring* and *lifting* techniques.

The work builds on recent topological deep learning tooling &mdash; DiffLift, TopoTune and
TopoModelX &mdash; and spans a range of architectures: CWN, GIN, GPS, GAN and CIN, among others.
