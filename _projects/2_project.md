**Ministry of the Economy and Finance &mdash; Directorate for Tax Studies and Statistics, data scientist intern (2025&ndash;2026)**

Built data-science models to estimate tax fraud and under-declaration from administrative data.

- **Modelling:** gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost) for the fraud / under-declaration signal.
- **Tuning:** Bayesian hyperparameter optimization improved the F1-score from **0.16 to 0.23**.
- **Explainability:** Shapley-value analysis on the boosted trees to interpret predictions and
  support the statistical reporting.
