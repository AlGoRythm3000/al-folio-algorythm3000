**Università di Pavia &mdash; deep learning project intern (2024)**

Developed and fine-tuned an object-detection pipeline to localize structural damage on
post-seismic images.

- Compared the **YOLOv8, v9 and v10** frameworks for damage localization.
- Fine-tuning and data curation improved **mean Average Precision by 0.16**.
- Tooling: `ultralytics`, PyTorch, TensorFlow.
