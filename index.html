Paris, France

gabriel.franco@ens-paris-saclay.fr

I am a machine learning student and research assistant. I hold a master’s degree from ENS Paris-Saclay in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering, and I am starting the IASD master’s in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Data Science (ENS Ulm / Mines Paris / Université Paris Dauphine-PSL). I was also admitted to the French Agrégation in Engineering and Computer Sciences (national rank 15/151).

The topic I worked the most on so far has been geometric and topological deep learning — graph neural networks, rewiring and lifting techniques, and the oversmoothing / oversquashing problems — which I currently work on as a research assistant at INRIA Saclay and CentraleSupélec. I have also worked on applied machine learning for tax-fraud estimation at the French Ministry of the Economy and Finance, on RNN-based control of an exoskeleton at Hublex, on post-seismic damage detection with YOLO at the University of Pavia, and on computer vision and reinforcement learning for autonomous racing.

Outside research I build Edge-AI pipelines on single-board computers, play the flute, race triathlons, and teach maths, physics and computer science.
