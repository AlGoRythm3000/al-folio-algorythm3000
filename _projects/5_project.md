**ENS Paris-Saclay &mdash; COVAPSY competition (2024)**

Guided a reduced-scale autonomous car around a track using onboard perception and control.

- **Computer vision** for lane and obstacle perception.
- **Reinforcement learning** for the driving policy.
- Vision-based spatial reasoning for autonomous navigation.
